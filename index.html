📖 User Guide & Instructions

🌌 1. 3D Galaxy Nebulae Structure

Your listening history is transformed into 6 distinct 3D Galaxy Nebulae: R&B, Jazz, Classical, Pop, Synthwave, and Rock. Each glowing pixel star represents a song you listened to.

🖱️ 2. Controls & Exploration

Left-drag to move through space. Right-drag to rotate the view. Scroll to zoom. Click a star to focus and open its details.

🎧 3. Spotify 30s Audio Preview & Spotify Link

Clicking a star opens its memory card with a 30-second Spotify audio preview button and a direct button to open the full track on Spotify.

📥 4. Custom Data Upload

Open Import Playlist, choose an Exportify CSV or Spotify JSON file, then generate your custom starfield.

🎵 5. Import a Spotify Playlist

CSV

Fastest: Exportify CSV

  1. Open Exportify, sign in to Spotify, and click Export beside the playlist you want.
  2. Upload the downloaded .csv file directly. Keep the first-row column names unchanged.
  3. Track URI, Track Name, and Artist Name(s) are recognized automatically; optional genre and audio-feature columns improve the visualization.
Open Exportify ↗
JSON

Advanced: Spotify API JSON

  1. In Spotify, open a playlist you own or collaborate on, choose Share → Copy link, then copy the playlist ID.
  2. Authorize Spotify's Get Playlist Items API, enter the playlist ID, and run the request.
  3. Paste the complete JSON response into Import Playlist, or upload it as a .json file.
  4. For more than 50 items, fetch additional offsets and combine them into one items array.
Spotify API ↗

CSV and JSON stay in your browser. Local files, podcast episodes, and market-restricted tracks may not have a playable Spotify link.
